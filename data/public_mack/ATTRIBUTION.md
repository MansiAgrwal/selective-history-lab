# Public category learning data attribution

Original creators: Michael L. Mack, Bradley C. Love and Alison R. Preston.

Mack, M. L., Love, B. C., & Preston, A. R. (2016). Dynamic updating of hippocampal object representations reflects new conceptual knowledge. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 113, 13203–13208. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1614048113

Source project: https://osf.io/5byhb/

License recorded by the OSF project: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The four source files in this directory are unchanged copies retrieved on 25 October 2026. Download addresses, sizes and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in outputs/public_category/provenance.json. The source README is retained as description.txt, including its original wording.

Derived files in outputs/public_category join the behavioral and timing records, add rule/period labels and summarize accuracy. These transformations were made for this project and are not endorsed by the original authors. Original data are provided as is, without warranties. No MRI volumes are included.
